## Design Context

### Users
Engineering leaders, technical recruiters, founders, and senior software peers evaluating Adam's technical leadership, systems judgment, and production impact. Their job is to understand his value quickly, inspect credible evidence, and decide whether to start a conversation.

### Brand Personality
Precise, assured, and experimentally technical. Software-first and operationally serious, with a bold identity that feels authored rather than templated.

### Aesthetic Direction
An operator console meets fashion editorial: a dark, cursor-responsive software signal field in the hero; oversized grotesque typography; technical micro-labels; warm-light case-study surfaces; and a dark contact finish. Scrolling stays conventional. Motion belongs to the hero interaction and individual project diagrams rather than overlays, sticky chapters, or scroll hijacking.

### Design Principles
- Lead with measurable software and leadership outcomes.
- Use one memorable interactive signal field instead of scattered visual effects.
- Make technical depth progressively discoverable without slowing scanning.
- Preserve legibility, performance, keyboard access, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Prefer authored diagrams, strong typographic composition, and local interaction over generic cards.
